## Projects

### The Seeds
The Seeds is an Unreal Engine game that focuses on environmental conservation and community. For a tomato, growing more tomatoes and fighting those trying to hurt them is part of making and protecting your own community. Growing trees is where the environmental conservation comes in. The robot enemies represent the greedy part of the world: they hoard any water in their path (a lore-accurate AI representation) and destroy communities in a far more direct way than in real life.
The entire gameplay was built through Blueprints, from planting and watering mechanics to health depletion and refilling, mud slowing pawns, and interacting with water and crops. Ifrah also worked with NavMesh for enemy patrolling and noise-based interactions, where things like searching for seeds could alert enemies. Enemies can take water and destroy crops, which makes them feel like a real threat. Ifrah modelled the tiny robot enemy in Blender and brought it into the game. The Seeds has a playable game build, and Ifrah posted videos and images about it on LinkedIn.

### Masketeer (2D Dual Reality Dungeon Crawler)
Made at Global Game Jam, Karachi (CEGA), 2nd place winner, January to February 2026. A 2D dungeon crawler where the player navigates a maze split between two parallel worlds. The Real World is full of locked doors and dead ends. The player uses a Mask mechanic to instantly switch into the Underworld to find secret paths, keys and potions. It includes a shooting system, enemy AI for Underworld-exclusive skeletons, and a trap system to balance high-reward exploration. Combat, scoring and item systems were all developed from scratch in under 48 hours. A playable build is available.

### Crimson Bastion (2D Sci-fi Platformer)
A Mars-based survival narrative game involving alien invasion and rescue objectives. Developed in Unity using C#, featuring combat mechanics, collectibles, a scoring system and win/lose states. Includes player shooting, enemy interactions and timed level progression, plus background music and sound effects. The Unity project is organised with scenes, scripts, prefabs and asset management. A playable build is available.

### Veil of Secrets (VOS) - 3D Horror Game
A first-person 3D horror exploration game made with Unity and C# for academic coursework. Features interactive systems such as doors, drawers, object pickups and environmental puzzles. Player interaction uses raycasting and event-based triggers. Audio, lighting and environmental effects create an immersive horror atmosphere, and a diary-based narrative system reveals the story through collectible items. It placed 4th among student projects at the departmental Game Exhibition. There is no playable build; it is shown through a LinkedIn post.

### Beaky Bounce (2D Game)
A Flappy-Bird-inspired 2D game written in C++ using SDL, with multiple levels, obstacles and interactive gameplay. It applies OOP concepts (encapsulation, inheritance, polymorphism, abstraction) to structure game entities. Includes power-ups, coins, gems, NPC interactions and animated bird states, multiple screens (splash, level, win/lose, character selection, settings), sound effects and texture management with SDL. There is no playable build; it is shown through a LinkedIn post.
